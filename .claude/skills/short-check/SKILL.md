---
name: short-check
description: Reviews a YouTube Short idea or draft before posting. Use when I share a title, hook or description for a gaming Short.
---

Review the Short using this checklist:
1. Hook: does the first 2 seconds show the funniest or most surprising moment?
2. Title: under 60 characters, specific, no clickbait that the video doesn't deliver.
3. Description: one line plus 3 relevant hashtags.
4. Fix: give one improved title and one improved hook line.

Keep the answer short and in simple language.

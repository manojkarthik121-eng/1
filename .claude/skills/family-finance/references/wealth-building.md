# Growing wealth: the honest playbook

Read this when someone asks how to get rich, make "infinite" or passive money, retire
early, or picks between "opportunities". The aim is to take their ambition seriously and
point it at the things that work.

## The wealth equation

Wealth = (Income − Spending) × Return^Time

Four levers, in the order they usually matter for a young family:

1. **Income**. Usually the biggest lever by far. A 30% raise from switching jobs, a
   certification, or a side business adds more in 3 years than a perfect portfolio does.
   Spend real attention here: what skills pay more in their field, is a job switch due
   (in India a switch often brings a 20-40% hike vs a 7-10% appraisal), can the
   non-earning or lower-earning spouse earn (tutoring, freelancing, home business,
   returning to work after a career break), can a hobby be sold?
2. **Gap (savings rate)**. Savings of 10% vs 30% of income is the difference between
   retiring at 65 and at about 50. Raise it on autopilot: whenever income goes up, put
   at least half of the raise into SIPs before lifestyle grows into it.
3. **Return**. Low-cost diversified equity over long periods. Costs and behaviour
   (panic-selling in crashes) matter more than fund picking.
4. **Time**. Starting 5 years earlier is often worth more than doubling the SIP later.
   Show this with `fincalc.py sip-fv` for "start now" vs "start in 5 years".

## Making "infinite money" concrete: financial independence

"Infinite money" in practice means **work becomes optional**: investments pay for the
family's life indefinitely. That number is concrete and reachable:

FI number ≈ 30-33 × yearly family spending (in today's rupees, inflated to the target year).

Example: a family spending ₹9 L/year needs about ₹2.7-3 Cr in today's money. Use
`fincalc.py fire` and then `fincalc.py goal` to get the monthly SIP. Showing the user
their own FI number and the date they'll hit it at their current pace and at a better
pace usually motivates them far more than generic advice.

Beyond FI, real fortunes come from **owning things that grow**: a business, equity, a
skill that scales (content, software, products), or a career with ownership upside. Help
the user think about which of these fits them, and point out the risk: most businesses
should be started with savings the family can afford to lose, after the emergency fund
and insurance are in place.

## Traps to name plainly, with numbers when possible

- **F&O / intraday trading**: SEBI's studies (FY22-FY24) found about 9 in 10 individual
  F&O traders lost money, with average losses of over ₹1 L per person per year.
  "Courses" and "tips" groups profit from the traders, not from the trades.
- **Guaranteed high returns** (2-5% a month), MLM, crypto "staking" schemes, ponzi
  apps, unregistered "investment advisers" on Telegram/WhatsApp. Check that any adviser
  is SEBI-registered (RIA) on sebi.gov.in.
- **Endowment/ULIP/money-back** policies sold as investment (see india-rules.md §4).
- **Lending to relatives/friends without a plan**. Gifts are fine; label them as gifts in
  the budget.
- **Lifestyle creep and EMIs for depreciating things** (phones, cars bought at more than
  half a year's income).
- **Over-concentration**: all money in one property, one stock (often the employer's), or
  gold jewellery.
- **Real-estate "investment" with a 2% rent yield on a 9% loan**.

Be warm but direct. If the statement shows betting/F&O/fantasy apps, call it out
explicitly with the yearly rupee total and what that money would become as a SIP over
10-15 years.

## Behaviour systems that make it stick

- **Pay yourself first**: SIP dates 1-2 days after salary credit; the budget is what's left.
- **Separate accounts**: salary → spending account (fixed monthly transfer) + goals
  account. Spending money that runs out is the budget.
- **Monthly 30-minute money meeting** for the couple/family: check the Budget sheet,
  tick the Action Plan, one decision per meeting.
- **Annual review**: step up SIPs, update net worth, recheck insurance cover, rebalance
  if equity/debt drifts by more than 5-10 points from target, harvest LTCG.
- **Teach the kids**: pocket money with a save/spend/give split.
